FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I have to switch model providers?
No. Nebula sits in front of whatever you already use — OpenAI, Anthropic, open-weights, or your own endpoint. Point your base URL at the gateway and everything keeps working, now with tracing, caching and guardrails on top.
How does usage-based pricing work?
You pay for requests through the gateway, not per seat. Every plan includes a generous monthly allotment; beyond that you're billed per thousand requests at a flat rate shown in your dashboard. No overage surprises — set hard caps and alerts.
Is my data used to train models?
Never. Nebula is a pass-through runtime. We don't train on your prompts or completions, and PII can be redacted before it ever leaves your perimeter. We're SOC 2 Type II certified with a signed DPA available on every paid plan.
Can I self-host or deploy in my own VPC?
Yes — Enterprise customers can run the full Nebula control plane inside their own cloud account or on-prem, with the same SDK and dashboard. Talk to sales for a deployment architecture review.
What languages and frameworks are supported?
First-class TypeScript and Python SDKs, plus a standard REST/OpenAPI surface for everything else. Drop-in adapters exist for LangChain, the Vercel AI SDK, and LlamaIndex.
