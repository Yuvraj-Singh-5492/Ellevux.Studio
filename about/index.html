The studio

Ellevux is a one-person web studio. I'm Yuvi.

I design and build the whole thing myself: the layout, the copy structure, the code, and the search and AI visibility work. There is no account manager between you and the person doing the work, because there is only the person doing the work.

One person means you talk to whoever is building your site. It also means I take a small number of projects at a time, so the dates I give you are real rather than optimistic.

I work remotely, in English, with owners anywhere. Everything runs over email and a live link you can open whenever you like, with one agreed call window a day if you want it.

No page builders. No agency silence.

Before you write

What I do not do.

Four things I turn down, listed here so you can rule me out in ten seconds rather than after an email.

  • Page builders, themes, or a template with your logo dropped in
  • White-label subcontracting to someone you never meet
  • Twelve-month lock-ins or a retainer you cannot leave
  • Work I cannot show you, or results I cannot prove

Questions

The things people ask before hiring me.

How much does a website cost?

A single strong page starts at 2,000 USD. A full site of up to six pages starts at 4,500 USD. A site with search and AI visibility work starts at 9,000 USD. Each figure is a starting point, and you get a written price for your own project within one business day.

Who owns the site, the domain and the files?

You do, all of it. The domain is registered in your name, the analytics live in your own account, and at handover you get the files, the logins and a short written guide to changing your own text. There is no retainer and no lock-in. If you stop working with me, you keep everything.

What if I do not like the design?

You see the real site at a real web address from the first week, not a picture of it, so nothing is a surprise at the end. Each package includes a named number of revision rounds. If the direction is wrong, we change it early, while changing it is cheap.

Can you work with the website I already have?

Yes. The 600 USD AI visibility audit is built for exactly that: what assistants currently say about your business, what is missing from your markup, and the ten things to fix in priority order. You can hand that list to anyone. You do not have to hire me to act on it.

You are one person. What happens if you are unavailable?

Being one person is why you talk to whoever is building your site. It also means I take a small number of projects at a time, so the dates I give you are real. You hold the domain, the hosting and the files throughout, so no part of your site is ever locked inside my accounts.

Where are you based, and how do we work across time zones?

I work remotely and take clients anywhere, in English. Before a project starts we agree a daily window where our working hours overlap, and that is when calls happen. Everything else runs over email and a live staging link you can open whenever you like.
